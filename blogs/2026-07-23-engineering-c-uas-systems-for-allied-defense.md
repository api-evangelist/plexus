Key Takeaways Military interoperability is moving from a high-level ambition to a technical requirement. The recent landmark agreement between the U.S. Department of War (DOW) and the United Kingdom’s Ministry of Defence (MoD) to standardize counter-unmanned aerial system (C-UAS) data represents a fundamental change in how defense technology will be procured and deployed.

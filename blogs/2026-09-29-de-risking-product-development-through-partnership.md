Key Takeaways Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) face constant pressure to launch new innovations without overspending. However, many organizations end up wasting time and resources managing a disjointed web of specialized, localized vendors. Scattering a product’s development across different design firms, suppliers and labs can create a disjointed process, leading to poor communication and costly delays....
